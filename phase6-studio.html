ROADMAP PRIORITY #1

Our main vision and customer promise

AI International Domain Center is not only a domain search site. It aims to be the easiest starting point for beginners to create and launch websites and applications, obtain domains, control their own branding, and get clear guidance.

  • Simple language and guided setup.
  • One dashboard for projects, domains, and support.
  • Customer-owned identity: no forced logo, watermark, ad, or platform credit.
  • Free or sponsored domains only when verified funding covers the actual cost.
  • Clear status, costs, ownership, export, backup, and renewal details.
